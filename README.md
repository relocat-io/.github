# relocat.io

Where is it actually good to live? We ask the people who lived there.

**[relocat.io/rating](https://relocat.io/rating/)** — a crowd-sourced rating of 1,465 cities and 199 countries
across 44 criteria: cost of living, rent, safety, climate, healthcare, schools, visas and residency,
internet, attitude to newcomers and more. Every score is 1–5 from someone who lived in the city,
weighted by how long they stayed and how recent the rating is.

- **Your own weights.** Pick what matters to you, and the ranking reorders around it.
- **Honest about uncertainty.** An AI estimate fills empty cells as a baseline, clearly marked,
  and gives way as people rate. Scores are adjusted for how many ratings stand behind them.
- **Open data.** All ratings and comments are available through a public API, no key required.
  There is also a skill for Claude Code, Codex and other AI agents.

Data for Russian cities (salaries from job listings, rent and housing prices, climate, air quality)
lives at [relocat.io](https://relocat.io).
